Personal Data Protection Policy

1. Introduction

Indian Cultural Association is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal data that we collect, process, and store. This Personal Data Protection Policy outlines our approach towards ensuring compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and safeguarding the rights of individuals in relation to their personal data.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all Members, Participants, contractors, and third parties working on behalf of Indian Cultural Association who have access to personal data in the course of their duties.

3. Principles

We adhere to the following principles in the collection, processing, and handling of personal data:

  • Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency: We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, ensuring individuals are informed about the purpose and lawful basis of processing.
  • Purpose Limitation: We collect and process personal data only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes.
  • Data Minimization: We only collect and process the minimum amount of personal data necessary for the intended purpose.
  • Accuracy: We strive to keep personal data accurate and up-to-date, taking reasonable steps to rectify inaccuracies.
  • Storage Limitation: We retain personal data for no longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Integrity and Confidentiality: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of personal data.
  • Accountability: We take responsibility for our data processing activities and demonstrate compliance with the PDPA.

4. Data Collection

We collect personal data for specified purposes and obtain consent where required. The purposes and methods of data processing are communicated to individuals at the point of collection.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

6. Data Subject Rights

We respect the rights of individuals under the PDPA, including the right to access, rectify, erase, and restrict the processing of their personal data. Requests to exercise these rights should be directed to the Data Protection Officer.

7. Data Breach Response

In the event of a data breach, Indian Cultural Association will promptly assess and report the breach to the relevant authorities and affected individuals, as required by law.

8. Data Protection Officer

Indian Cultural Association has appointed a Data Protection Officer who is responsible for overseeing compliance with this policy. The Data Protection Officer can be contacted at: indianculturalassociation@gmail.com

9. Review and Updates

This policy will be reviewed periodically and updated to ensure ongoing compliance with the PDPA and any other applicable data protection laws.

10. Contact Information

For any questions or concerns regarding this policy or our data protection practices, please contact: indianculturalassociation@gmail.com
